👍 What families praise (Hancock Park)

  • Staff "care and investment in students… doing their best while balancing high ambitions."
  • Genuine diversity — race, culture, religion, socioeconomic — teachers attuned to individual needs.
  • Rich programs: Inner City Arts, Astro Camp, movie-making, gardening, drama (Shakespeare, musicals).
  • Welcoming, active parent community.

👎 What families flag

  • Larger class sizes (growth outpacing staffing at times).
  • Mixed teacher quality — "some good, some not qualified."
  • One review criticized the principal (careless / yelling).
  • Neighborhood high school (Fairfax) is weak — plan private or magnet for 9–12.
Read for our kids: Hancock Park Elementary is a strong, warm, diverse K-5 — good for Emilie and Jasmine's early years. But the K-12 path weakens toward high school, unlike Santa Monica's consistently strong Franklin → Lincoln → Samohi. Budget for private/magnet later, or plan to move before high school.

Diligence — before any offer

  • Ask why it's underwater — is it just a market/greed story, or are there defects deterring buyers? Get inspection despite "new construction."
  • New-construction builder warranty — what's covered, is it transferable?
  • Traffic noise + air — visit at rush hour; measure noise from La Cienega/Wilshire. Consider MERV-13 whole-house filtration cost.
  • Verify the assigned school boundary (Hancock Park vs another LAUSD school) with LAUSD locator.
  • Pool/spa equipment age + maintenance cost.
  • Permits/CofO for the 2023 build; any open items.
