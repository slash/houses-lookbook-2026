Diligence — before any offer

  • Confirm the added bedroom / +227 sqft was permitted (request the SM Residential Building Record).
  • HOA reserve study + budget + 12 mo minutes + special-assessment history (4-unit HOA, $918/mo).
  • Santa Monica soft-story seismic retrofit status — 1981 + subterranean garage. Interior reno does NOT retrofit the structure.
  • Master insurance policy adequacy + condo warrantability (CA insurance crisis).
  • Elevator service contract — who maintains, who pays.
  • Pull the full listing price history — days-on-market + any cuts already taken.
