⚠️ Why I don't trust the clean fire result

Laurel Canyon is textbook Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone territory — steep terrain, continuous chaparral, narrow single-access roads. An automated hit against the CAL FIRE LRA layer returned "not in zone," which is more likely a bad layer match than a real exemption. It is being verified against LA City's own VHFHSZ layer, LAFD's fire-zone map, and ZIMAS. Do not treat this as clear. Insurance cost and even insurability hang on it.

✅ Crime is genuinely excellent

🎓 Schools — one strong signal, pending confirmation

Wonderland Avenue Elementary is 0.30 miles away

Wonderland Ave is one of LAUSD's most sought-after elementary schools (~9/10). If this address is zoned for it, that is a material advantage over Sunswept's Dixie Canyon (8/10, ELA 67.9% / Math 60.9%).

But proximity ≠ enrollment. The Sunswept research proved this exactly: that house missed Carpenter Elementary by 0.26 miles and landed in a school with a 16–21 point lower proficiency rate. Boundary verification is running now via the LAUSD Resident School Identifier.
